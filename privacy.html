Mindmap Infinite Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy describes information handled by the Mindmap Infinite Android app and this website.

Information stored on your device

Maps, cards, settings, and related files are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Provider credentials you choose to save are encrypted with an Android Keystore key. Android backup is disabled for the app. Exported files are separate copies; protect them wherever you save or share them.

The app does not send your maps to a Mindmap Infinite-operated server. It does not include advertising or analytics. Diagnostics stay on your device unless you choose to copy or share them.

Information sent to services you choose

  • AI providers: When you connect an AI service and request an AI feature, the app sends the prompt and the context or attachments selected for that request directly to the provider you chose, using the credential you configured. The provider processes that information under its own terms and retention policy. The app does not control how that provider uses or retains it.
  • Update checks: Update checking is enabled by default and checks GitHub Releases. GitHub receives the ordinary network request information needed to serve that request. You can turn update checking off in the app's About settings.
  • Model and content downloads: If you choose a model or other download, the request goes to the selected publisher or download host. That service may receive ordinary connection information.
  • Speech input: If you invoke Android speech recognition, the selected Android speech-recognition service handles the audio and its own privacy terms apply.
  • Exports and sharing: If you export or share information, the destination you choose receives the content you send.
  • This website: The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information to deliver the pages under its own privacy terms. The site loads its own static assets and has no advertising, analytics, or account sign-in.

Network calls to third parties are initiated by the corresponding feature or setting. Review the relevant provider's privacy terms before sending sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Your local maps and settings remain on your device until you delete them or clear or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes app-private local data; exported copies are not removed. To request deletion of data held by an AI or download provider, contact that provider directly.

Children

Mindmap Infinite is a general-purpose productivity app and is not designed specifically for children. AI providers may have their own age requirements.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. For privacy requests, use the developer contact email shown on the Google Play listing. For general questions, use the website's public GitHub Issues page; do not post personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
