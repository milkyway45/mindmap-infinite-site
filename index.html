Good questions

A few things to know.

What is Mindmap Infinite?

It’s an Android mind mapping app for turning ideas, questions, and plans into connected cards you can explore and organize.

Can I use it without AI?

Yes. AI is optional. Create, branch, organize, search by words, and export maps without connecting an AI provider.

Where are my maps stored?

Your maps stay in the app’s private storage on your device. If you ask a connected AI for help, the selected prompt and context go directly to that provider. Read the full privacy policy.

Is Mindmap Infinite available on Google Play?

The Android Google Play release is coming soon. Follow website updates on GitHub.
